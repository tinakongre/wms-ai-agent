Warehouse Operations

Warehouse staff should regularly monitor inventory levels.

Products below their reorder level should be reviewed for replenishment.

When prioritizing replenishment, staff should consider the difference between current quantity and reorder level.

Inventory quantities represent the currently available units recorded in the warehouse inventory system.

Warehouse W01 primarily contains keyboard and mouse inventory.

Warehouse W02 contains monitor inventory.

Warehouse W03 contains mechanical keyboard inventory.
