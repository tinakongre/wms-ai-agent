Warehouse Reorder Policy

A product should be reordered when its available quantity falls below its reorder level.

The reorder level is the minimum recommended stock quantity for a product.

If the available quantity is below the reorder level, the product is considered low on stock and should be reviewed for replenishment.

Warehouse staff should prioritize products with the largest gap between their current quantity and reorder level.

For example, if a product has 15 units available and its reorder level is 50 units, the product is low on stock and should be considered for reorder.

Inventory quantities are maintained in the warehouse inventory database.
